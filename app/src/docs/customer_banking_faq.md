# Customer Banking FAQ

Example frequently asked questions from customers. For demo purposes only.

---

## Accounts & Balances

### How do I check my account balance?
You can view your current balance by signing in to online banking or the mobile app and selecting the account you want to check. Your available balance and pending transactions are shown on the account overview page.

### Why is my available balance different from my current balance?
Your **current balance** includes all posted transactions. Your **available balance** subtracts pending payments, holds, and reserved amounts (for example, card authorizations). Funds may take one to two business days to become fully available after a deposit.

### How do I open a new savings account?
Sign in, go to **Accounts → Open a new account**, choose Savings, and follow the on-screen steps. Most applications are approved instantly if you already have a checking account with us.

### Can I have joint accounts?
Yes. Both account holders must complete identity verification. Either joint owner can view balances, transfer funds, and manage the account online.

## Transfers & Payments

### How do I transfer money between my accounts?
Go to **Transfers → Between my accounts**, select the from and to accounts, enter the amount, and confirm. Internal transfers usually post immediately.

### How long do external transfers take?
Transfers to accounts at other banks typically take **1–3 business days**. Same-day transfers may be available for a fee before the daily cutoff time shown in the app.

### How do I set up a recurring bill payment?
Open **Payments → Pay a bill**, select or add the payee, enter the amount, choose a schedule (weekly, monthly, or custom), and confirm. You can edit or cancel upcoming payments anytime before the processing date.

### Is there a daily transfer limit?
Yes. Standard personal accounts have a daily outgoing transfer limit. You can view your current limits under **Settings → Limits**. Contact support if you need a temporary increase.

### Can I cancel a transfer after I submit it?
Internal transfers that have already posted cannot be reversed online. External transfers may be canceled if they are still pending. Check **Activity → Pending** for cancel options, or contact support as soon as possible.

## Cards & Spending

### How do I activate my new debit card?
Sign in to the app, go to **Cards**, select the new card, and tap **Activate**. You will be asked to confirm the last four digits of the card number and set a PIN.

### What should I do if my card is lost or stolen?
Freeze the card immediately under **Cards → Freeze card**, then contact us to request a replacement. Freezing blocks new purchases and ATM withdrawals while keeping your account open.

### How do I change my PIN?
In the mobile app, go to **Cards → Manage PIN** and follow the prompts. For security, you may need to verify your identity with a one-time code sent to your registered phone or email.

### Why was my card declined?
Common reasons include insufficient available funds, a merchant outside your spending region, a temporary fraud hold, or an expired card. Check **Cards → Recent activity** and notifications for details.

### Can I set spending limits on my card?
Yes. Under **Cards → Controls** you can set daily purchase limits, block ATM withdrawals, and restrict online or international transactions.

## Online & Mobile Banking

### I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password**, enter your username or registered email, and follow the link we send you. You will create a new password and may need to confirm a one-time code.

### How do I enable two-factor authentication?
Go to **Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication** and choose text message or authenticator app. We recommend enabling this for all online banking access.

### Is the mobile app available on iOS and Android?
Yes. Download our official app from the Apple App Store or Google Play. Always confirm the publisher name matches our bank before installing.

### Can I deposit a check with my phone?
Yes. Open **Deposit → Mobile check deposit**, photograph the front and back of the endorsed check, enter the amount, and submit. Funds availability depends on the amount and your account history.

## Fees & Statements

### Where can I find my monthly statement?
Go to **Statements**, choose the account and month, and download the PDF. Statements are typically available within a few business days after the statement period closes.

### Are there monthly maintenance fees?
Fee details depend on your account type. Many checking accounts waive the monthly fee when you meet minimum balance or direct-deposit requirements. See **Accounts → Fees** or your account agreement for specifics.

### How do I dispute a transaction I do not recognize?
Open the transaction in **Activity**, select **Report a problem**, and follow the prompts. Provide as much detail as possible. We will review the claim and may temporarily credit your account while we investigate.

## Support

### How do I contact customer support?
Use in-app **Help → Contact us** for secure messaging, call the number on the back of your card, or visit a branch during business hours. For suspected fraud, use the 24/7 fraud hotline listed in the app.

### What information should I have ready when I call?
Have your full name, date of birth, account or card number (last four digits are often enough), and a recent transaction amount ready so we can verify your identity quickly.
